01

What is RicozChange?

RicozChange is a web app for IT change management: the process a team follows before anything risky touches production — a database migration, a server upgrade, a config change.

Instead of approvals buried in email threads and meetings, every change is filed in one place, scored for risk with clear reasons, approved by the right people, and checked afterwards so the team learns from every change.

RicozChange tells you how risky a change is, why, what it can break, and gets it approved in minutes instead of days.

The problem it solves

The old wayThe RicozChange way
Risk is a gut feeling in a meetingExplainable risk score — every change gets a 0–100 score with line-by-line reasons
Two teams change the same system the same night and find out during the incidentThe collision detector warns at filing time
CAB meetings review everything, so everything is slowLow-risk changes auto-approve in one click; only risky ones need people
Approvers miss requests in emailApprovals arrive as Slack DMs with Approve / Reject buttons
Nobody remembers if the last change actually workedThe post-change check ("did it work?") feeds a live failure-rate dashboard

Who it's for

  • Engineers — file changes fast, get AI-drafted rollback and test plans, see their change's risk before submitting.
  • Approvers / CAB — decide from Slack or the approval inbox with the full "why" inline.
  • Managers — freeze calendars, CAB meetings, imports and the failure-rate dashboard.
  • Auditors / compliance — an append-only audit log of every action, by whom, when.

How a change flows through the product

Seven steps. Every one of them is built and running today.

1
Draft
Engineer files a change — web form, CSV import, or demo seed data.
2
Score
Risk engine scores 0–100 and shows every reason: +70 major change, +15 business-critical system, −25 rollback plan.
3
Check
Collision detector — overlap with other changes? freeze period? bad timing?
4
Approve
Low risk → auto fast-track (one click). High risk → Slack DMs / approval inbox; CAB reviews majors.
5
Schedule
Safe-window finder suggests the best slot around freezes and neighbouring changes.
6
Implement
Owner runs the change in its window, helped by AI-drafted rollback & test plans.
7
Learn
"Did it work?" → success / partial / failed. Results feed the failure-rate dashboard… and future risk scores (same system failed before? the score goes up).
✓
Every step is audit-logged
Who did what, when — written once, never edited.

What the risk score looks at (all visible, no black box)

FactorExample line item the approver sees

Rules the app enforces & who can do what

Hard rules (all covered by automated tests)

  • You can never approve your own change.
  • Engineers can create and submit changes but cannot approve anything — the server returns 403 and logs the attempt.
  • Every action lands in the audit log with a real user identity — denials included.
  • Slack button clicks are idempotent; invalid signatures get 401 plus an audit entry.
  • Slack down? Delivery failures are recorded and web approvals keep working.
  • No rollback plan? The score says so — and it costs points.

The 3-minute demo script (use this with your manager)

  1. Dashboard — failure-rate KPI, volume and trend charts.
  2. Open change #7 — walk the "Why this score" panel line by line.
  3. Approvals — approve it with one click (or from a real Slack DM).
  4. Simulator — drag a slider; watch score, collisions and blast radius react live.
  5. Back on the change: implement → complete → post-change check → dashboard CFR updates.

Two-sentence pitches

To an engineer: "You file the change once; RicozChange tells you the risk, warns about collisions, drafts your rollback plan, and asks you afterwards whether it worked."
To a manager: "Every change is scored with reasons, risky ones get real approvals in Slack, low-risk ones stop wasting CAB time — and you finally get a real failure-rate number."
